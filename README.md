Posts and Beams
=================

Overview
--------

Adds large wooden beams and posts of varying log types, which can help prevent cave-ins similar to support beams.

 - Posts have a support factor of 6, while beams have a support factor of 6. For reference, vanilla beams have a support factor of 3.

 - Place with shift to put down a single post or beam section; place without sneaking to auto-place posts and beams.


Config Settings (`VintageStoryData/ModConfig/PostsAndBeams.json`)
--------

 - `MaxDistanceBeamFromPostBlocks`: How far away a beam can be from a post when connected; defaults to `3`.


Known issues
--------

 - Wildcraft Trees lacks debarked textures for most woods, currently. You might see plank textures on beams that use Wildcraft wood.
